## 4. FIXTURE DESIGN (fresh, pre-registered before any candidate call)

180 fresh fixtures, generated by script, gold via the established dual
rule-based derivation protocol (frozen V2.2 contract + frozen V2.7E
uncertainty derivation; stored verdicts excluded from derivation input):

- forbidden: 60 (including 12 safety-critical)
- route: 60
- uncertainty: 60, including 24 fresh repair-boundary fixtures covering the
  preregistered V2.7E boundary classes (contradictory/garbled/self-retracted
  limitation -> UNRESOLVED; UNCLEAR_PROSE -> UNRESOLVED)

Coverage must include, per the V2.8 diagnostic: at least 20 uncertainty
fixtures where the correct verdict is UNRESOLVED despite a superficially
plausible definite reading (the overcommitment trap class), distributed
across PARTIAL/SATISFIED/VIOLATED distractors.

Hard zero tolerance: exact-text collisions against all historical fixture
corpora (>= 4117 texts) = 0; disputed fixtures are discarded and replaced
with new IDs, never repaired in place.

## 5. CALIBRATION PHASE (burned, before official run)

Separate synthetic calibration set, minimum 24 fresh fixtures, never reused
for official validation. Prompt/arbitration design must implement the
preregistered UNRESOLVED-forcing principle: explicit textual signals
(self-retraction, contradiction, garble, unclear prose, hedged-to-vague) are
first-class UNRESOLVED-forcing requirements in the prompt and arbitration
layer, not incidental capabilities.

Maximum 3 preregistered prompt iterations, measured only on the burned
calibration set. Iteration 1 must be generated BEFORE any candidate call and
frozen. No iteration after official fixture exposure.

The reported metric unresolved_overcommit_rate (gold-UNRESOLVED rows committed
to a definite verdict) is REPORT-ONLY in V2.9. No new numeric gate is
introduced; the gate set remains identical to V2.8.
